# Plan — making the loop's record as good as its mechanism

Proposal, 2026-11-24, written while round 17 was being dispatched. Reviewed
against `doc/Loop.md` (round 15 status, 258 lines), `doc/Loop-queue.md`,
`doc/loop/round-15.md`, `doc/loop/round-16.md` (`9fe231b`), `git log`, the
worktree list, and one measurement (`bin/man-diff --section 8 --max 0`: 358
pages, 16 s, so the whole corpus is about three minutes).

Written by a fresh agent with no round history. The agent that has been
*placing* the rounds may write its own plan beside this one
(`plan-2026-11-24-loop.md`); this file is deliberately shaped to be comparable
with it: a claim, the evidence, the phases, what proves each, what it costs,
and the decisions that are not the agent's.

## The claim

The mechanism is sound and the *record* is not. The failures below are all one
of five kinds: `main` verified after it is written; durable knowledge kept in a
session; a rule left as prose where a gate is cheap; state a resumer must trust
rather than derive; a queue that cannot feed itself.

## What to keep

Disjoint file sets; rebase and re-prove per lane; park, never reset; the suite
at a pinned tip in a throwaway worktree; the queue's `files` column; numbers
that name their tip and their population; a scheduler that is an agent rather
than a script. None of these is the problem, and the "rules that earned their
place" section is the most valuable thing in the file.

## Evidence, as of 13:40Z

- `main` = `9fe231b`. Round 16's three lane commits (`9ba3175`, `c2b32ec`,
  `f3f1539`) sat on `main` with no round record from 13:26 to 13:39. The record
  arrived only because the run was still alive; a run that died in that window
  would have left a fresh resumer to re-dispatch p58, p51 and p60.
- `doc/Loop.md:3` said "round 15" before and after `9fe231b` rewrote the rest
  of the loop docs. The hand-typed status line is stale *inside the commit that
  updated everything else*: it is the one field that cannot be maintained by
  hand.
- Round 16's first merge attempt hung for the full 15 minutes of `round.sh`'s
  budget: a lane's proof command (`npx mocha test/man-viewer.mjs` without
  `--exit`) printed 158 passing and never exited. The merge step has no budget
  of its own and no way to tell "hanging" from "working" (`round-16.md`).
- `round.sh`, `lanes-up.sh` and `rules.md` have never been committed
  (`git log --all -- '*round.sh'` is empty) and live in a per-session `$LOOP`.
- Round 15's record points at `$LOOP/p50-crash/a2-wip.patch`; every previous
  run's worktree root is now prunable. The patch is gone, which contradicts
  `doc/Loop.md:70-72` ("losing `$LOOP` costs the briefs and the claims, not the
  work").
- No lock exists. `$LOOP` is per-session, so two loops cannot see each other,
  yet the protocol explicitly shares `main` with other agents and the user.

## Generality

About 15 of 258 lines are mechanism; the rest are instances: the lane table
hard-codes `js/man-parse.mjs` and `js/man.mjs`, the proofs are `mocha` and
`groff -Tutf8`, the measurement is a `man-diff` sweep, the ids are `p<n>` with
"no corpus occurrence" verdicts.

The mechanism transfers almost for free. What does not transfer is the supply
engine: groff is a reference implementation, which is why this loop yields
about 50 of the repo's 183 man commits. Outside man there is no reference, so
item supply falls back to a scout lane and to Matt's bug reports, and red-first
needs a declared proof kind.

Per area:

- `ext/code` fits best — `test/fake-oc.mjs` and `test/code-events.mjs` give
  offline deterministic proofs, and the opencode SDK is a spec to be red
  against.
- The editor core needs `exclusive:` on shared styling and keybinding files,
  and on e2e: electron e2e cannot run three-wide, so those rounds are two lanes.
- Every area needs its own `measure:` or the round record loses the one honest
  number that makes a long run comparable with itself.

## Phases

One commit each, between rounds, gated by the suite plus `test/loop.mjs`.
Phases 1–4 by hand (they rewrite the merge step the loop stands on); 5–7 the
loop can do to itself.

### 0. Make the record true — done during this plan

`9fe231b` writes `round-16.md`, the five-section sweep and the queue's statuses.
Two residuals:

- The `Status:` line in `doc/Loop.md` still names round 15. Either derive it
  (Phase 3) or delete it — a field that is wrong after the commit that updates
  everything else should not exist.
- p50's preserved patch was lost with the session. Say so in the queue, so the
  next lane rewrites it instead of looking for it.

**Proved by:** `git log -1` agrees with `Loop.md`, and no queue row is
dispatchable for a commit already on `main`.

### 1. Track the tool and the contract (2 h, behaviour unchanged)

`bin/loop.mjs` (`lanes-up | round | claim | park | status | pick`),
`doc/loop/rules.md` (the lane contract, from `$LOOP/rules.md`),
`doc/loop/brief.md` (skeleton), `.opencode/command/loop.md` (`/loop` with the
deadline as an argument). Ported verbatim first, then improved.

Node rather than bash because `bin/*.mjs` is already linted by `npm run check`,
`bin/check-layering.mjs` ignores `bin/`, and `test/man-diff.mjs` already
imports an extensionless bin script, so the test precedent exists. `$LOOP` then
keeps only `state`, `claims/`, `lane-<id>.{md,out}` and `round-N.log` — genuinely
scratch, so losing it costs a round's prose and nothing else.

**Proved by:** one round runs from the tracked tool. **Bootstrap:** the current
`$LOOP/round.sh` stays in place until it has.

### 2. An atomic round (3 h)

Cut `loop/r<n>` from `main`; rebase and re-prove each lane onto it; run the
suite, the check and the measurement there; write `doc/loop/round-<n>.md` onto
it; then one `git merge --ff-only loop/r<n>` in the canonical checkout, with
the `index.lock` retry and a per-step `timeout` that says what it waited on.

- `main` only ever moves in whole, verified rounds.
- A dead run leaves an unmerged integration branch, not a half-round on `main`.
- A user commit mid-round costs one rebase and one suite run, not a per-lane
  race.
- `loop branch <name>` becomes free, if the target ever stops being `main`.

**Proved by:** SIGKILL a round; `main` is unchanged, `loop/r<n>` is intact, and
`status` says "unmerged round, finish or discard".

### 3. State you can derive instead of trust (3 h)

- `Loop-item: <id>` on lane commits; `Loop-round: <n>` and `Loop-verify: <tip>`
  on the round commit.
- `bin/loop.mjs status` prints the lock and its heartbeat, the last verified
  tip, `main` against it, the restored-from-trailers done set, the ready set
  after the filters, and the parked branches. `--check` fails on a disagreement
  between the queue and the trailers; `--write` refreshes the queue's status
  block between markers.
- Lock and heartbeat in `$(git rev-parse --git-common-dir)/bred-loop.lock`,
  shared by every session and worktree, stale after 20 minutes of no
  heartbeat, `--force` to break.

**Proved by:** `test/loop.mjs` on a scratch repo (trailers, disagreement, stale
lock). **Done when** deleting `$LOOP` loses only briefs and logs.

### 4. Gates (3 h)

- **Files gate:** the commit's paths must equal the brief's `owns:` list. This
  is the only invariant the design rests on and nothing checks it today.
- **Red gate:** in the verify worktree at `base`, check out the lane's test
  files from its branch, require the proof to fail, then require it to pass
  after the rebase. `RED: exempt <reason>` for fix-and-fixture-in-one-file
  cases, logged loudly. Round 16's hanging proof is the argument for the third
  state: neither red nor green, so the gate needs a timeout and says "did not
  finish".
- **Lane report block**, as the last lines of the report, captured to
  `$LOOP/lane-<id>.out`:

      ITEM: p58
      BRANCH: agent/bred-<ws>-a1
      TIP: 9ba3175
      FILES: js/man-roff.mjs test/man-parse.mjs
      PROOF: npx mocha test/man-parse.mjs --grep hla
      RED: 1

**Proved by:** `test/loop.mjs` showing all four outcomes: green merge,
unclaimed-file park, not-red park, conflict park without a reset.

### 5. Remove the man-isms from the protocol (2 h)

`bin/loop.mjs pick` derives three disjoint sets from the queue (status, dirty,
hot, parked, exclusive filters) and prints the plan. The man prefixes stay as a
hint, not a definition. The queue gains `area`, `proof:` (`test | golden |
measure | verdict`) and `exclusive:` — which is how "at most one item touching
`js/man-parse.mjs`" becomes a rule rather than a lane, and generalises to e2e
and to shared styling. Lane stacking lets one lane take several small items in
order, one commit each, which drains the a1 backlog of no-corpus items without
another agent.

**Proved by:** a round dispatched from a queue containing a non-man item, with
no edit to `doc/Loop.md`.

### 6. A supply engine (half a day, per area)

- **Scout lane:** read-only, no claim, no merge slot, dispatched when the ready
  set is under three. Its deliverable is a queue row — a symptom, a file set,
  a proof command — which is exactly the unpaid `triage` and `next` work.
- **`bin/man-fuzz-diff`:** reuse the deterministic seeds and pools in
  `test/man-fuzz.mjs`, render the candidate with groff, minimise the
  disagreement with ddmin, print a reproduction and the word delta. Bounded per
  the load rule (`timeout`, `nproc-2`, no `while :`), and never during a
  round's suite window.
- **A diff-tool gold set:** a rule change ships a test showing it still reports
  a genuinely lost word. p60 and p62 were the measuring device being wrong, and
  `test/man-diff.mjs` is the right place to start.

**Proved by:** a round whose items came from the harness, each failing at base.

### 7. Scheduling and housekeeping (1 h)

- Round 16 already measured all five sections; make that the rule and record
  the cost, instead of "one measurement per round" buying stale sections
  (`doc/Loop.md:221-223`). Measure alone, after the lanes, with the tool's own
  output quoted in the record.
- Flake quarantine: a tracked list skipped by default, run by its own script,
  never parking a lane. p50 is the case study: one lane died hunting a flake
  and the patch went with it.
- Backfill `doc/loop/round-12..14.md` or drop the per-round-file convention —
  rounds 12–14 are a table row only.
- `status` should list the `agent/*` branch litter that is safe to delete.

## Order and cost

1→4 are one session and strictly ordered. 5 follows 4. 6 and 7 are per-area and
repeatable. Phases 2–4 rewrite the merge step, so they are committed between
rounds with the old script still available and proved by a killed round rather
than by reasoning.

## Open decisions (Matt's)

1. Does `main` keep moving in the background, or does the loop live on a branch
   that is fast-forwarded when you sit down? Phase 2 is identical either way;
   only the last step differs, and the answer decides whether Phase 2 is a fix
   or a simplification.
2. How much of the record do you actually read? If the round files go unread,
   Phase 7 should delete the convention rather than backfill it.
3. Was round 16's unrecorded half-hour a real cost, or a non-event? If it was a
   non-event, Phase 2 drops to a nice-to-have and Phases 1, 3 and 4 carry the
   plan on their own.
